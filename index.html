About Us

Valorant is a 5v5 tactical shooter developed by Riot Games, which prioritizes a player-centric approach by fostering a fair, safe, and thriving community. The game's "about us" is best understood through Riot's core principles, which focus on hyper-serving core players, being part of the community, fostering a fair and inclusive environment, and supporting players globally. Valorant itself is a game of outwitting opponents with a mix of unique agent abilities and precise gunplay.

How to play?

  • Player-centric philosophy: Riot Games, the developer, centers its design and development around the player experience.
  • Community involvement: Riot aims to be part of the community by listening to players, engaging with them, and making them feel a sense of ownership.
  • Community involvement: Riot aims to be part of the community by listening to players, engaging with them, and making them feel a sense of ownership.
  • Fostering a thriving ecosystem: The company strives to create a fair, safe, and welcoming environment for all players.
  • Supporting players globally: Riot invests in initiatives that benefit players around the world, regardless of their country or culture.

About the game, Valorant

  • Gameplay: It is a 5v5 tactical shooter where one team attacks and the other defends.
  • Characters: Players control "agents," each with their own unique abilities.
  • Objective: Rounds are won by eliminating the opposing team, planting or defusing the "spike" (the objective).
  • Platforms: The game is available on PC and is also coming to Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5.
